01 Logo

One mark, always white.

The logo is always white on a coloured background: light teal, teal dark, ink or a photo with a veil. It never sits on white or on a light background, and there is no black/ink logo: never use it. On light teal use the version with shadow, on darker backgrounds the plain white one.
